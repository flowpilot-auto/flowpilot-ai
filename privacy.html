PRIVACY NOTICE

How enquiry details are intended to be handled.

This is a starter privacy notice for the demo website. It should be reviewed before the trial form is connected to live data collection.

Current demo status

The trial form on the current demo website does not transmit or store submitted information. It only demonstrates the intended form experience.

When live submissions are enabled

The intended purpose of collecting a name, business name, email, phone number, business type and automation request is to respond to the enquiry, assess whether a trial may be suitable and communicate about the requested service.

Data handling

Only information reasonably needed for the enquiry should be collected. Access should be limited to the people and services required to respond to the request and operate the workflow.

Third-party services

Live automation may rely on third-party services for form delivery, workflow automation, email, spreadsheets, hosting or payments. This notice should be updated with the actual providers before production use.

Payments

No payment details are currently collected by this website. If payments are added later, payment-card details should be handled by the chosen payment provider rather than stored directly by Fumba Automations.

Contact and changes

A business contact email and any legally required privacy details should be added here before the website begins collecting real customer information.

Important: this page is a practical website starter, not legal advice. Update it to match the actual services, tools and legal requirements that apply when the business goes live.
