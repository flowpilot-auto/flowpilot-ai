PRIVACY NOTICE

How enquiry details are handled.

This notice explains how information submitted through the Fumba Automations trial form is used in the current trial-request process.

Information collected

When you submit the trial form, Fumba Automations collects the name, business name, email address, phone number (if provided), business type and the description of the process you would like to automate.

Why the information is collected

The information is used to receive and record your enquiry, send a confirmation email, assess whether a trial may be suitable, contact you about the request and manage the trial process.

Data handling

Trial requests are processed through the Fumba Automations workflow and recorded in a lead-management spreadsheet so the enquiry can be followed up. Access should be limited to the people and services needed to respond to the request and operate the workflow.

Services used

The current setup uses GitHub Pages to host the website, Cloudflare to route form submissions, n8n to process the workflow, Google Sheets to store trial-request records and Gmail to send internal notifications and confirmation emails. These services may process information as part of providing those functions.

Payments

No payment details are collected through the trial-request form. If you choose to continue with a paid setup after a trial, payment arrangements will be provided separately.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the service, website or connected tools change. The information on this page should reflect the way trial requests are actually handled.

Note: this is a practical privacy notice for the current trial-request setup and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed as the business, connected services and legal requirements develop.
