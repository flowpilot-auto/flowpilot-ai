FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Fumba Automations is aimed at service businesses where enquiries, bookings, follow-ups or admin repeat often enough to benefit from a structured workflow.

Is this just an AI chatbot?

No. AI may be used inside a workflow when it genuinely helps, but the service is broader automation: capturing information, routing it, notifying people, updating records and connecting business tools.

Do I need to replace the tools I already use?

Not necessarily. The aim is to work around the business's current process where practical. The exact setup depends on the tools and access available.

How does the trial work?

You describe one process you want to improve. A focused workflow can then be demonstrated or trialled before you decide whether to continue with a paid setup.

Do I need to pay to request a trial?

No. The trial request is free. If you decide to continue after the trial, the final setup, ongoing support and payment details are agreed directly before launch.

What does ongoing support include?

That depends on the agreed service. It can include monitoring the workflow, troubleshooting integrations and making agreed minor adjustments.
