Turn new enquiries into an organised job pipeline.

Fumexalade helps trade businesses capture job details, record leads, acknowledge customers and surface the enquiries that need attention.

THE WORKFLOW

One enquiry. Less chasing details.

A focused trade workflow can collect the information you normally ask for manually and move it into one simple process.

01

Customer submits details

Name, phone, location, job type, description and timeframe can be captured through a simple form.

02

Lead is recorded

The enquiry is added to a CRM or lead sheet so it does not disappear inside messages or inboxes.

03

Both sides get an update

The customer receives a confirmation while the business receives the job details for review and follow-up.
